Design Choices

I built this page using a single accent color, green, in the idea to mimic the green of a tennis court. The single accent also makes the page seem seamless and smooth without distracting your eyes in 30 different directions. Since I made the web page the focus of a 2026 tennis season I wanted the data and pictures work together and not create the sensation of individualized sections. I started by placing the pictures first side by side, as an introductory element of the charts. The charts were the main attraction so I made them bigger and stacked them, so the focus of attention would be one at a time. The other factor while creating this web page was keeping the information closer together so it's easy for the eye to move section by section. To do so I enlarged the borders to keep the focus in the middle of the page and provided small but not divading titles as the page continues.
